Grainbee: A Quantum-Resistant Blockchain-Based Ration Distribution System with Hardware Security Modules

Authors

Sohel Ahmed JoniRabiul RahatNishat TasninPartho GhoseM. Rashid

Abstract

Grainbee presents a quantum-resistant blockchain-based ration distribution system that utilizes Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) to prevent corruption in food subsidy programs. The system employs raft-consensus permissioned blockchain technology.
